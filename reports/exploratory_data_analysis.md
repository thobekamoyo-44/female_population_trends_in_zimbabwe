# Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## 1. Introduction

Exploratory Data Analysis was performed to understand patterns and trends in women's reproductive health indicators in Zimbabwe.

The analysis focused on changes over time and differences across wealth quintiles.

Python, Pandas and Matplotlib were used to calculate summary statistics and create visualisations.

## 2. Female Population Analysis

The female population was examined across decades to understand population growth.

The average female population increased from approximately 2.23 million in the 1960s to 8.50 million in the 2020s.

A line chart was used to illustrate this trend.

## 3. Fertility Analysis

### Fertility Trends Over Time

The average total fertility rate was calculated for each decade.

The results showed:

- 1990s: 4.25 births per woman
- 2000s: 3.94 births per woman
- 2010s: 4.24 births per woman

A line chart was used to visualise changes in fertility over time.

### Fertility Across Wealth Groups

Fertility rates were also compared across five wealth quintiles.

A horizontal bar chart was used to highlight differences between wealth groups.

The poorest quintile recorded a higher average fertility rate than the richest quintile.

## 4. Contraceptive Use Analysis

Contraceptive use among married women was examined across decades.

The average percentages were:

- 1990s: 50.20%
- 2000s: 62.33%
- 2010s: 63.87%

A line chart was used to show changes over time.

Contraceptive use was also compared across wealth quintiles.

A scatter plot was used to explore its relationship with fertility rates. A strong negative correlation of approximately -0.97 was observed across the five wealth groups.

This indicates an association, not proof of causation.

## 5. Healthcare Accessibility Analysis

The analysis examined the percentage of women reporting difficulty accessing healthcare because of distance to a health facility.

The decade averages were:

- 1990s: 33.36%
- 2000s: 44.86%
- 2010s: 36.86%

A line chart was used to illustrate the changes.

Healthcare accessibility was also compared across wealth groups, revealing substantial differences between the poorest and richest quintiles.

## 6. Age at First Marriage Analysis

The median age at first marriage was examined across wealth quintiles.

The results showed that women in wealthier groups generally married later than women in poorer groups.

A horizontal bar chart was used to compare the five wealth groups.

## 7. Teenage Pregnancy and Motherhood Analysis

Teenage pregnancy and motherhood among women aged 15–19 were examined across wealth quintiles.

The results showed considerable differences between wealth groups:

- Poorest quintile: 31.52%
- Richest quintile: 9.34%

A horizontal bar chart was used to display these differences.

The indicator was also examined across decades to identify changes over time.

## 8. Visualisation Techniques

The following visualisation techniques were used:

| Chart Type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Line charts | Examine trends over time |
| Horizontal bar charts | Compare wealth quintiles |
| Scatter plot | Explore the relationship between fertility and contraceptive use |

## 9. Summary

The exploratory analysis revealed differences in reproductive health outcomes across wealth groups and changes in selected indicators over time.

The results provided the foundation for the key findings, recommendations and conclusions presented in the accompanying report.
